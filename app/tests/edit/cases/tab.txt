Tab and Shift+Tab (docs/EDIT-MODE.md §7.8), and the Tab rules of §6.5: list items nest and come out, a paragraph after
a list item joins it, code is indented, cells are stepped through with their text selected. Format as in enter.txt.

=== tab_nest_item (§7.8: nested under the item before it; the first of a nested ordered list is numbered 1)
src: 1. a\n2. ‸b\n
do: Tab
want: 1. a\n   1. ‸b\n

=== tab_nest_bullet (§7.8: a bullet item gains the item before's content column)
src: - a\n- ‸b\n  more\n
do: Tab
want: - a\n  - ‸b\n    more\n

=== tab_nest_joins_sublist (§7.8: after a nested list the item joins it, its number kept)
src: 1. a\n   1. x\n2. ‸b\n
do: Tab
want: 1. a\n   1. x\n   2. ‸b\n

=== tab_first_item_nothing (§7.8: no item before it in its list: nothing)
src: - ‸a\n- b\n
do: Tab
want: - ‸a\n- b\n

=== tab_items_selected (§7.8: every selected item nests)
src: - a\n- ⟦b\n- c⟧\n
do: Tab
want: - a\n  - ⟦b\n  - c⟧\n

=== stab_outdent (§7.8: one level out; the items after it become its children, numbered from 1)
src: 1. a\n   1. x\n   2. ‸y\n   3. z\n2. b\n
do: S-Tab
want: 1. a\n   1. x\n2. ‸y\n   1. z\n2. b\n

=== stab_outdent_bullet (§7.8: a nested bullet item, its continuation lines too)
src: - a\n  - ‸b\n    more\n
do: S-Tab
want: - a\n- ‸b\n  more\n

=== stab_top_item (§7.8: at the top level the item becomes a paragraph, as Backspace on a first item)
src: - ‸a\n- b\n
flags: nolist
do: S-Tab
want: ‸a\n\n- b\n

=== stab_top_middle_item (§7.8: a middle item: blank lines part it from the items on both sides)
src: - a\n- ‸b\n- c\n
flags: nolist
do: S-Tab
want: - a\n\n‸b\n\n- c\n

=== tab_para_into_item (§7.8, UX-1: a paragraph after a list item joins it as a paragraph of its own)
src: - a\n\n‸b\nc\n
flags: nolist
do: Tab
want: - a\n\n  ‸b\n  c\n

=== tab_para_nothing (§6.5, §7.8: Tab in any other paragraph does nothing)
src: abc\n\n‸def\n
flags: nolist
do: Tab
want: abc\n\n‸def\n

=== tab_heading_nothing (§7.8: nor in a heading)
src: # ‸Head\n
flags: nolist
do: Tab
want: # ‸Head\n

=== stab_para_nothing (§7.8: Shift+Tab in a paragraph does nothing)
src: - a\n\n‸b\n
flags: nolist
do: S-Tab
want: - a\n\n‸b\n

=== tab_code (§7.8: four blanks at the caret in code)
src: ```\nco‸de\n```\n
do: Tab
want: ```\nco    ‸de\n```\n

=== tab_code_tabs (§7.8: a tab where the block's indentation uses tabs)
src: ```\n\tx\nco‸de\n```\n
do: Tab
want: ```\n\tx\nco\t‸de\n```\n

=== tab_code_lines (§7.8: a selection over several lines: each line indented after the container prefix)
src: ```\n⟦ab\ncd⟧\n```\n
do: Tab
want: ```\n    ⟦ab\n    cd⟧\n```\n

=== stab_code (§7.8: Shift+Tab takes up to four blanks off the line's start)
src: ```\n      co‸de\n```\n
do: S-Tab
want: ```\n  co‸de\n```\n

=== stab_code_tab (§7.8: or one tab)
src: ```\n\tco‸de\n```\n
do: S-Tab
want: ```\nco‸de\n```\n

=== tab_cell_next (§7.8, UX-24: the next cell, its text selected)
src: | a‸ | bc |\n|---|---|\n
do: Tab
want: | a | ⟦bc⟧ |\n|---|---|\n

=== tab_last_cell (§7.8: after the last cell a new row, the caret in its first cell)
src: | a | b‸ |\n|---|---|\n
do: Tab
want: | a | b |\n|---|---|\n| ‸ |  |\n

=== stab_cell_prev (§7.8: the previous cell, its text selected)
src: | ab | ‸c |\n|---|---|\n
do: S-Tab
want: | ⟦ab⟧ | c |\n|---|---|\n

=== stab_first_cell_nothing (§7.8: before the first cell nothing)
src: | ‸ab | c |\n|---|---|\n
do: S-Tab
want: | ‸ab | c |\n|---|---|\n

=== tab_phantom_after_item (§6.7, §7.8: a phantom after a list item takes the item's continuation prefix)
src: - a\n- ‸\n
flags: nolist
do: Enter; Tab; type "x"
want: - a\n\n  x‸\n
