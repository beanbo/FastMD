Links, the private clipboard format and the source popups (docs/EDIT-MODE.md §8.3, §7.11, §9.2; Phase 3b): Ctrl+K on a
selection, at the caret and in a link of each kind, Remove link, the balanced slice FastMD copies for itself, and a
popup's text written back into the object it edits (AtomSource: an image index, or b<block> for an object of its own;
then the field). src holds the caret ‸ (or a selection ⟦ … ⟧); want the source afterwards; clip what Copy took. Each
case also runs with CRLF line ends, inside a quote and inside a list item unless its flags say otherwise.

=== link_selection (§8.3: the selection becomes the link's text; it stays selected)
src: see ⟦the docs⟧ now
do: Link("https://x.org/a")
want: see [⟦the docs⟧](https://x.org/a) now

=== link_dest_blanks (§8.3: an address with blanks in <…>)
src: ⟦file⟧ here
do: Link("my file.md")
want: [⟦file⟧](<my file.md>) here

=== link_dest_parens (§8.3: parentheses that balance stay bare, one that does not needs <…>)
src: ⟦a⟧ b
do: Link("x(1)")
want: [⟦a⟧](x(1)) b

=== link_dest_unbalanced (§8.3)
src: ⟦a⟧ b
do: Link("x)")
want: [⟦a⟧](<x)>) b

=== link_strips_inner (§8.3: links inside the selection lose their delimiters)
src: ⟦a [b](u) c⟧ d
do: Link("v")
want: [⟦a b c⟧](v) d

=== link_grows_over_bold (§8.3: a span half inside comes in whole)
src: x **bo⟦ld** te⟧xt
do: Link("u")
want: x [**bo⟦ld** te⟧](u)xt

=== link_escapes_brackets (§8.3: a bracket of the text is escaped)
src: ⟦a]b⟧ c
do: Link("u")
want: [⟦a\]b⟧](u) c

=== link_caret_inserts (§8.3: no selection, no link: the address linked to itself)
src: see ‸here
do: Link("https://a.b")
want: see [https://a.b](https://a.b)‸here

=== link_edit_dest (§8.3: in an inline link only the destination changes)
src: a [li‸nk](old "t") b
do: Link("new")
want: a [li‸nk](new "t") b

=== link_edit_empty_dest (§8.3)
src: a [li‸nk]() b
do: Link("new")
want: a [li‸nk](new) b

=== link_ref_needs_confirm (§8.3: a reference link's definition changes only once the notice was confirmed)
src: a [li‸nk][r] b\n\n[r]: old\n
do: Link("new")
state: refused=refdef
want: a [li‸nk][r] b\n\n[r]: old\n

=== link_ref_confirmed (§8.3: the definition line gets the address)
src: a [li‸nk][r] b\n\n[r]: old\n
do: Link!("new")
want: a [li‸nk][r] b\n\n[r]: new\n

=== link_ref_shortcut (§8.3: [t] is defined by t, case and blanks aside)
src: a [Li‸nk] b\n\n[link]: old\n
do: Link!("new")
want: a [Li‸nk] b\n\n[link]: new\n

=== link_edit_autolink (§8.3: an autolink with another address becomes a link)
src: see <http://a‸.b> now
do: Link("http://c.d")
want: see [http://a‸.b](http://c.d) now

=== unlink_inline (§8.3)
src: a [li‸nk](u) b
do: Unlink
want: a li‸nk b

=== unlink_ref_keeps_def (§8.3: the definition stays)
src: a [li‸nk][r] b\n\n[r]: u\n
do: Unlink
want: a li‸nk b\n\n[r]: u\n

=== unlink_autolink_url (§8.3: escaped where it would link again)
src: see <http://‸x.org> now
do: Unlink
want: see http\://‸x.org now

=== unlink_autolink_mail (§8.3)
src: mail <a@b‸.co> now
do: Unlink
want: mail a\@b‸.co now

=== unlink_permissive_www (§8.3)
src: go www.ex‸ample.com now
do: Unlink
want: go www\.ex‸ample.com now

=== unlink_nothing (§8.3: no link at the caret)
src: a‸ b
do: Unlink
state: refused=link
want: a‸ b

=== copy_balanced_bold (§7.11: `**br⟦own** fox⟧` → `**own** fox`)
src: **br⟦own** fox⟧ end
do: Copy
clip: **own** fox
want: **br⟦own** fox⟧ end

=== copy_inside_bold (§7.11: a span around the selection around the slice too)
src: a **b⟦c⟧d** e
do: Copy
clip: **c**
want: a **b⟦c⟧d** e

=== copy_part_of_link (§7.11)
src: a [li⟦nk te⟧xt](u) b
do: Copy
clip: [nk te](u)
want: a [li⟦nk te⟧xt](u) b

=== copy_items_whole (§7.11: blocks covered whole keep their markers)
src: - ⟦one\n- two⟧\n
do: Copy
clip: - one\n- two
want: - ⟦one\n- two⟧\n

=== copy_heading_and_text (§7.11)
src: # ⟦Title\n\npara⟧graph\n
do: Copy
clip: # Title\n\npara
want: # ⟦Title\n\npara⟧graph\n

=== copy_paste_private (§7.11: pasted inside FastMD it keeps its markup)
src: **br⟦own** fox⟧ end\n\nnext\n
do: Copy; click(17,1); PastePrivate
clip: **own** fox
want: **brown** fox end\n\nnext**own** fox‸\n

=== atom_formula (§9.2: an inline formula's TeX)
src: ‸a $x$ b
tex: on
do: AtomSource(0,0,"y+1")
want: ‸a $y+1$ b

=== atom_formula_one_line (§9.2: its line ends become blanks)
src: ‸a $x$ b
tex: on
do: AtomSource(0,0,"p\nq")
want: ‸a $p q$ b

=== atom_formula_block (§9.2: the lines between `$$` and `$$`, each with the block's prefix)
src: ‸para\n\n$$\nx\n$$\n
tex: on
do: AtomSource(b1,0,"a\nb")
want: ‸para\n\n$$\na\nb\n$$\n

=== atom_formula_block_one_line (§9.2)
src: ‸para\n\n$$x$$\n
tex: on
do: AtomSource(b1,0,"z")
want: ‸para\n\n$$z$$\n

=== atom_diagram (§9.2: the content lines)
src: ‸p\n\n```mermaid\ngraph TD\n```\n
tex: on
do: AtomSource(b1,0,"graph LR\n    A --> B")
want: ‸p\n\n```mermaid\ngraph LR\n    A --> B\n```\n

=== atom_diagram_fence (§9.2: a line that would close the fence makes both fences longer)
src: ‸p\n\n```mermaid\ngraph TD\n```\n
tex: on
do: AtomSource(b1,0,"A\n```\nB")
want: ‸p\n\n````mermaid\nA\n```\nB\n````\n

=== atom_html (§9.2: an HTML block's lines)
src: ‸p\n\n<div>\nx\n</div>\n
do: AtomSource(b1,0,"<div>\ny\n</div>")
want: ‸p\n\n<div>\ny\n</div>\n

=== atom_front (§9.2: front matter's lines)
src: ---\ntitle: a\n---\n\n‸text\n
flags: noquote nolist
do: AtomSource(b0,0,"title: b\nx: 1")
want: ---\ntitle: b\nx: 1\n---\n\n‸text\n

=== atom_front_dashes (§9.2: a --- line would end it: refused)
src: ---\ntitle: a\n---\n\n‸text\n
flags: noquote nolist
do: AtomSource(b0,0,"x: 1\n---")
state: refused=front
want: ---\ntitle: a\n---\n\n‸text\n

=== atom_image_path (§9.2, §8.8: the path as a destination)
src: ‸a ![cat](c.png) b
do: AtomSource(0,1,"my cat.png")
want: ‸a ![cat](<my cat.png>) b

=== atom_image_path_angle (§9.2: a destination in <…> is replaced with its brackets)
src: ‸a ![cat](<my c.png>) b
do: AtomSource(0,1,"c.png")
want: ‸a ![cat](c.png) b

=== atom_image_alt (§9.2: a bracket of the alt text is escaped)
src: ‸a ![cat](c.png) b
do: AtomSource(0,0,"big]cat")
want: ‸a ![big\]cat](c.png) b

=== image_several (§8.8: several pictures, one after the other)
src: a ‸b
do: Image("x.png\ny.png","x\ny")
want: a ![x](x.png) ![y](y.png)‸b
