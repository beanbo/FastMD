Where typed text goes: SrcOfText(MAP_CARET) at a text boundary (docs/EDIT-MODE.md §6.3, the "Typing x gives" table,
and §6.3.1). Each case: src holds the caret ‸ at a source offset; TextOfSrc turns it into a text position (the drawn
caret), and SrcOfText(MAP_CARET) must give want's ‸ - where an x typed there would be written. The typing itself is
Phase 2a; this pins the mapping it will stand on.

=== caret_bold_end (§6.3 **bold‸** tail: typing continues the bold)
src: **bold‸** tail\n
do: caret
want: **bold‸** tail\n

=== caret_before_bold (§6.3 plain ‸**bold**: outside the bold)
src: plain ‸**bold**\n
do: caret
want: plain ‸**bold**\n

=== caret_block_start_bold (§6.3 ‸**Bold** at the block start: inside the enterable opener)
src: ‸**Bold** text\n
do: caret
want: **‸Bold** text\n

=== caret_before_link (§6.3 see ‸[Docs](u))
src: see ‸[Docs](u)\n
do: caret
want: see ‸[Docs](u)\n

=== caret_link_end (§6.3 [Docs‸](u) more: never inside a link from its right edge)
src: [Docs‸](u) more\n
do: caret
want: [Docs](u)‸ more\n

=== caret_block_start_link (§6.3 ‸[Docs](u) at the block start: outside the link)
src: ‸[Docs](u) are\n
do: caret
want: ‸[Docs](u) are\n

=== caret_before_code (§6.3 run ‸`make`)
src: run ‸`make`\n
do: caret
want: run ‸`make`\n

=== caret_code_end (§6.3 `make‸` now: past the code closer)
src: `make‸` now\n
do: caret
want: `make`‸ now\n

=== caret_link_in_bold (§6.3 **[x‸](u)** y: past the link, still inside the bold)
src: **[x‸](u)** y\n
do: caret
want: **[x](u)‸** y\n

=== caret_bold_in_link (§6.3 [**x‸**](u) at the block end: past the link and the bold in it)
src: [**x‸**](u)\n
do: caret
want: [**x**](u)‸\n

=== caret_formula_end (§6.3 the $E$‸ is: after the formula's closer)
src: the $E$‸ is\n
do: caret
want: the $E$‸ is\n

=== caret_formula_end_notex (§6.3 the same, formula as code text: past the closer too)
src: the $E‸$ is\n
tex: off
do: caret
want: the $E$‸ is\n

=== caret_block_start_link_in_bold (§6.3 ‸**[x](u)**: inside the bold, outside the link)
src: ‸**[x](u)** y\n
do: caret
want: **‸[x](u)** y\n

=== caret_after_escape (§6.3 after an escape atom)

=== caret_after_emoji (§6.3 after an emoji atom)

=== caret_soft_break (§6.3 at the left edge of a soft break: before it; §6.6)
src: one‸\ntwo\n

=== caret_after_soft_break (§6.3 at the right edge of a soft break: the next line's content)

=== caret_heading_start (§6.3 a heading's start: after its marker)

=== caret_empty_atx (§6.3.1 an empty ATX heading: after the #s)
