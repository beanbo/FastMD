Inserts and table operations (docs/EDIT-MODE.md §8.8, §8.9): a table, a formula, a diagram, a picture, a rule - after
the current block with blank lines on both sides, each line with the block's continuation prefix, or in a phantom
row that becomes them; rows and columns added, deleted and aligned on the rows' pipes. src holds the caret ‸ (or a
selection ⟦ … ⟧); want the source afterwards; phantom where a phantom row stands then. Each case also runs with CRLF line
ends, inside a quote and inside a list item unless its flags say otherwise.

=== table_after (§8.8: after the block, the caret in the first header cell)
src: abc‸\n
do: Table(2,3)
want: abc\n\n| ‸ |  |  |\n| --- | --- | --- |\n|  |  |  |\n

=== table_default_rows (rows include the header row)
src: abc‸\n
do: Table(3,2)
want: abc\n\n| ‸ |  |\n| --- | --- |\n|  |  |\n|  |  |\n

=== table_before_next (a blank line after it too, when the next line is not blank)
src: a‸\nb\n
flags: nolist noquote
do: Table(1,1)
want: a\nb\n\n| ‸ |\n| --- |\n

=== table_between (blank lines on both sides, the next block untouched)
src: a‸\n\nb\n
flags: nolist
do: Table(1,1)
want: a\n\n| ‸ |\n| --- |\n\nb\n

=== table_in_cell_after (§8.1: in a cell: after the table)
src: | x‸ |\n| --- |\n
do: Table(1,1)
want: | x |\n| --- |\n\n| ‸ |\n| --- |\n

=== table_phantom (§8.8: in a phantom the phantom becomes the table)
src: abc‸\n
flags: nolist
do: Enter; Table(1,2)
want: abc\n\n| ‸ |  |\n| --- | --- |\n

=== formula_inline (§8.8: `$x$` at the caret, a blank apart from a letter)
src: cost‸\n
flags: nolist
tex: off
do: Formula
want: cost $x$‸\n

=== formula_selection (§8.8: the selection in one block as its TeX)
src: a ⟦b+c⟧ d\n
tex: off
do: Formula
want: a $b+c$‸ d\n

=== formula_block (§8.8: `$$`, `x`, `$$` after the block)
src: abc‸\n
tex: off
do: FormulaBlock
want: abc\n\n$$\n‸x\n$$\n

=== formula_block_atom (with TeX the block is an object: the caret on it, at its first line's start)
src: abc‸\n
tex: on
flags: nolist noquote
do: FormulaBlock
want: abc\n\n‸$$\nx\n$$\n

=== diagram_flowchart (§8.8: a Mermaid block of template 0)
src: abc‸\n
tex: off
flags: noquote nolist
do: Diagram(0)
want: abc\n\n```mermaid\n‸flowchart TD\n    A[Start] --> B{Choice}\n    B -->|Yes| C[Do it]\n    B -->|No| D[End]\n```\n

=== diagram_mindmap (template 7: nesting by two more blanks)
src: abc‸\n
tex: off
flags: noquote nolist
do: Diagram(7)
want: abc\n\n```mermaid\n‸mindmap\n    root((Idea))\n      Branch A\n      Branch B\n```\n

=== hr_after (§8.8, UX-3: a rule after the block, a phantom row after the rule with the caret in it)
src: abc‸\n
do: Hr
want: abc\n\n---‸\n
phantom: after 1 in

=== hr_then_type (typing in that phantom: a paragraph after the rule)
src: abc‸\n
do: Hr; type "x"
want: abc\n\n---\n\nx‸\n

=== hr_phantom (in a phantom: the rule, then the phantom after it)
src: abc‸\n
flags: nolist
do: Enter; Hr
want: abc\n\n---‸\n
phantom: after 1 in

=== image_inline (§8.8: a picture in the line at the caret)
src: see ‸\n
do: Image("a.png","pic")
want: see ![pic](a.png)‸\n

=== image_after_code (§8.8: after a code block: a paragraph of its own - a picture block, the caret on it)
src: ```\nco‸de\n```\n
flags: nolist noquote
do: Image("a.png","pic")
want: ```\ncode\n```\n\n‸![pic](a.png)\n

=== table_row_above (§8.9)

do: Table.RowAbove

=== table_row_above_header_refused (§8.9: never above the header)

do: Table.RowAbove

src: | a | b |\n| --- | --- |\n| c‸ | d |\n
do: Table.RowBelow
want: | a | b |\n| --- | --- |\n| c‸ | d |\n|  |  |\n

=== table_row_below_header (§8.9: under the header: after the delimiter row)
src: | a‸ | b |\n| --- | --- |\n| c | d |\n
do: Table.RowBelow
want: | a‸ | b |\n| --- | --- |\n|  |  |\n| c | d |\n

=== table_col_left
src: | a | b |\n| --- | --- |\n| c‸ | d |\n
do: Table.ColLeft
want: |  | a | b |\n| --- | --- | --- |\n|  | c‸ | d |\n

=== table_col_right
src: | a | b |\n| --- | --- |\n| c‸ | d |\n
do: Table.ColRight
want: | a |  | b |\n| --- | --- | --- |\n| c‸ |  | d |\n

=== table_col_right_no_trailing_pipe (§8.9: a row without a trailing pipe gets one)
src: a | b\n--- | ---\nc | d‸\n
do: Table.ColRight
want: a | b |  |\n--- | --- | --- |\nc | d‸ |  |\n

=== table_col_left_no_leading_pipe (a row without a leading pipe gets `|  |` at its start)
src: a | b\n--- | ---\nc‸ | d\n
do: Table.ColLeft

=== table_short_row (§8.9: a row too short to have the cell is left alone)
src: | a | b |\n| --- | --- |\n| c |\n| d | e‸ |\n
do: Table.ColRight
want: | a | b |  |\n| --- | --- | --- |\n| c |\n| d | e‸ |  |\n

=== table_del_row (§8.9: the caret to the same column of the row that takes its place)

do: Table.DelRow
want: | a | b |\n| --- | --- |\n| e | ‸f |\n

=== table_del_last_row

=== table_del_col

=== table_del (§8.9: the whole table; the caret to the end of the block before)

=== table_del_only_block (nothing before it: a phantom where it was)
