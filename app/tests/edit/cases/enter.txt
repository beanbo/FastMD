Enter, Shift+Enter and Ctrl+Enter (docs/EDIT-MODE.md §7.6), the phantom rows they make and how typing makes those real
(§6.7), soft and hard breaks under them (§6.6). src holds the caret ‸ (or a selection ⟦ … ⟧); `do` lists the operations
(`Phantom` puts the caret into the phantom row, as ↑ / ↓ do in the app); want is the source after them with the caret
where the glue puts it (normalised: where typed text would go - so before a link's `[`, a code span's backtick); phantom
is where a phantom row stands afterwards (the block it is next to, `in` when the caret is in it). Each case also runs
with CRLF line ends, inside a quote and inside a list item unless its flags say otherwise.

=== enter_para (§7.6 paragraph: ab‸cd → ab⏎⏎‸cd)
src: ab‸cd\n
flags: nolist
do: Enter
want: ab\n\n‸cd\n

=== enter_second_para (§7.6: the same as the second paragraph of a loose item in the list variant)
src: x\n\nab‸cd\n
do: Enter
want: x\n\nab\n\n‸cd\n
