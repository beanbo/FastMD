Backspace at the start of a block and Delete at its end (docs/EDIT-MODE.md §7.7): the matrix of what happens there -
headings and list items give up their marks, quotes a level, text blocks are joined, code, tables and objects are
stepped onto - and §7.5's normalisation of what meets where text went (spans of one kind merge, blanks leave a span).
Format as in enter.txt; each case also runs with CRLF, in a quote and in a list item unless flagged.

=== bs_atx_to_para (§7.7: an ATX heading's marks go; a paragraph, escaped where it would be a list, §7.4)
src: # ‸1. Intro\n
do: BS
want: ‸1\\. Intro\n

=== bs_atx_closing (§7.7: the closing sequence goes too)
src: ## ‸Title ##\n
do: BS
want: ‸Title\n

=== bs_setext_to_para (§7.7: a setext heading's underline goes)
src: ‸Title\n===\n
do: BS
want: ‸Title\n

=== bs_empty_atx (§7.7: an empty heading's line and one blank line go; the caret to the end before)

=== bs_item_loose (§7.7, F16: in a loose one the item before gets a paragraph)
