Map goldens, blocks (docs/EDIT-MODE.md §4.4, block table). Each map: block is the whole map of its source, as
fastmd-edit-tests --update writes it and a reviewer checked it: b<n> = a block and its source record (line, content
beg..end, the end of its last content line, the end of its last line) with the source slices «prefix⟨content⟩rest|
outer»; s = its segments (text t+len, kind, source); span = its spans; c<n> = containers; img<n> = picture ranges;
order = the blocks in source order.

=== map_paragraph (§4.4 paragraph)
src: First line\nsecond line\n\nAnother paragraph.\n
map:
  b0 P - line=0 beg=0 end=22 le=22 outer=22 "First line second line" «⟨First line\nsecond line⟩»
    s 0+10 P 0..10 «First line»
    s 10+1 TEXT 10..11 «\n» " "
    s 11+11 P 11..22 «second line»
  b1 P - line=24 beg=24 end=42 le=42 outer=42 "Another paragraph." «⟨Another paragraph.⟩»
    s 22+18 P 24..42 «Another paragraph.»
  order 0 1
