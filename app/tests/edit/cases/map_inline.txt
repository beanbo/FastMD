=== map_emoji (§4.4 an emoji shortcode splits a chunk: PLAIN / TEXTATOM / PLAIN)
src: Go :rocket: now, :notanemoji: stays and `:code:` too.\n
map:
  b0 P - line=0 beg=0 end=53 le=53 outer=53 "Go 🚀 now, :notanemo…tays and :code: too." «⟨Go :rocket: now, :notane… stays and `:code:` too.⟩» nostop=4
    s 0+3 P 0..3 «Go »
    s 3+2 TEXT 3..11 «:rocket:» "🚀"
    s 5+29 P 11..40 « now, :notanemoji: stays and »
    s 34+6 P 41..47 «:code:»
    s 40+5 P 48..53 « too.»
    span CODE t34..40 o40..41 c47..48 «`»«`»
  order 0
